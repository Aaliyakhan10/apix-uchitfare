1. The Problem Statement & Industry Reality (The "Why")

In India, the official Consumer Price Index (CPI) measures inflation for goods and services across urban and rural sectors. Historically, airfare indices have been captured via manual point-in-time quotations collected by field investigators at airline booking counters or travel agent storefronts once a month.

The Three Structural Crises in Aviation Inflation Measurement:
  1. Algorithmic Dynamic Pricing: Over 90% of domestic air tickets in India are sold online through real-time revenue management algorithms. Fares fluctuate by 200% to 400% based on lead time (\(T+1\) emergency vs \(T+45\) advance purchase), holiday surge, and time of day. Infrequent counter quotes fail to represent actual consumer expenditure.
  2. The Regulatory Impasse (March 2026): When the DGCA and MoSPI requested airlines to provide proprietary fare bucket (RBD) data feeds, the Federation of Indian Airlines (FIA) declined, citing commercial confidentiality and competitive sensitivity.
  3. The APIx Breakthrough: APIx (UchitFare) provides a non-invasive, statistically certified, fully autonomous system that harvests multi-carrier quotes every 30 minutes, eliminates web scraping noise and class leakages via machine learning, computes traffic-weighted Laspeyres price relatives, explains price shocks via SHAP, and flags route monopolies—without requiring airline cooperation.

3. Mathematical Formulations & Econometric Methodologies

The APIx system implements rigorous mathematical formulations adhering to official MoSPI CPI guidelines and the United Nations System of National Accounts (SNA).

3.1 Composite Route Daily Fare Formulation

To reflect real-world consumer behavior across advance purchase intervals, fares for route \(r\) on date \(t\) are aggregated across five booking horizons \(W = \{T+1, T+7, T+15, T+30, T+45\}\) using empirical weights \(W_w\):

\[P_{r,t} = \sum_{w \in W} \left( P_{r,t,w} \cdot W_w \right) \quad \text{where} \quad \sum_{w \in W} W_w = 1.00\]
Booking Horizon (\(w\)) Lead Time Window Calibrated Weight (\(W_w\)) Economic Rationale
\(T+1\) 24 Hours (Emergency) 0.15 Captures extreme last-minute algorithmic surge markups
\(T+7\) 7 Days (Tactical Business) 0.30 Highest volume domestic booking window in India
\(T+15\) 15 Days (Standard) 0.25 Balanced pricing benchmark across competitive carriers
\(T+30\) 30 Days (Vacation) 0.20 Advance leisure purchase window; reduced dynamic volatility
\(T+45\) 45 Days (Early Bird) 0.10 Baseline capacity release fares without dynamic surcharges

3.2 Route Price Relatives Formulation

\[\text{PR}_{r,t} = \frac{P_{r,t}}{P_{r,0}}\]

Where \(P_{r,0}\) is the certified base period fare for route \(r\) (normalized to base year \(2026 = 100.0\)).

3.3 Traffic-Weighted Laspeyres Airfare Price Index (APIx)

MoSPI's Consumer Price Index standard mandates the Laspeyres Price Relative Index with fixed base-period expenditure and traffic weights. The headline APIx is calculated as:

\[I_{\text{APIx}}^{(t)} = \sum_{r=1}^{N} \left( \frac{P_{r,t}}{P_{r,0}} \cdot W_r^{\text{traffic}} \right) \times 100\]

Where \(W_r^{\text{traffic}}\) is calculated from official DGCA annual Passenger-Kilometers (\(\text{PKM}\)):

\[W_r^{\text{traffic}} = \frac{\text{PKM}_r}{\sum_{j=1}^N \text{PKM}_j} = \frac{\text{Passengers}_r \times \text{Distance}_r}{\sum_{j=1}^N (\text{Passengers}_j \times \text{Distance}_j)} \quad \text{with} \quad \sum_{r=1}^N W_r^{\text{traffic}} = 1.0000\]

3.4 Sub-Indices Formulations

  • Metro Corridors Sub-Index: \(I_{\text{Metro}}^{(t)} = \frac{\sum_{r \in \text{Metro}} \left( \text{PR}_{r,t} \cdot W_r^{\text{traffic}} \right)}{\sum_{r \in \text{Metro}} W_r^{\text{traffic}}} \times 100\)
  • Regional & UDAN Sub-Index: \(I_{\text{Regional}}^{(t)} = \frac{\sum_{r \in \text{UDAN}} \left( \text{PR}_{r,t} \cdot W_r^{\text{traffic}} \right)}{\sum_{r \in \text{UDAN}} W_r^{\text{traffic}}} \times 100\)
  • 7-Day Rolling Moving Average: \(\text{MA}_7(t) = \frac{1}{7}\sum_{k=0}^{6} I_{\text{APIx}}^{(t-k)}\)

3.5 Superlative Index Formulations (Neutralizing Substitution Bias)

During surges, consumers substitute away from expensive flights to cheaper alternatives. The Laspeyres index with fixed weights can overstate inflation. APIx incorporates the Methodology Recalculator:

  • Jevons Geometric Mean Index:
    \[I_{\text{Jevons}}^{(t)} = \prod_{r=1}^N \left( \frac{P_{r,t}}{P_{r,0}} \right)^{W_r^{\text{traffic}}} \times 100\]
    Result: Accommodates price elasticity of substitution, producing an index \(\approx 1.8\%\) lower than Laspeyres.
  • Fisher Ideal Superlative Index:
    \[I_{\text{Fisher}}^{(t)} = \sqrt{I_{\text{Laspeyres}}^{(t)} \cdot I_{\text{Paasche}}^{(t)}}\]

3.6 Data Reliability & Statistical Confidence Score

MoSPI requires continuous quality auditing for web-scraped feeds:

\[\text{Confidence Score}_t = \min \left( 100.0, \frac{N_{\text{actual}}(t)}{N_{\text{expected}}(t)} \times 100 \right) \quad \text{where} \quad N_{\text{expected}} = \sum_{r=1}^{25} \left( |\text{Carriers}_r| \times 5 \text{ windows} \right)\]

Uptime Thresholds: \(\ge 90\%\) (Optimal), \(80\% - 89.9\%\) (Acceptable), \(< 80\%\) (Degraded / Trigger Auto-Recrawl).

3.7 ML Anomaly Detection: Scikit-learn Isolation Forest

Outliers are screened across a 3D numerical subspace: \(\mathbf{x} = \left[ \text{fare\_per\_km}, \frac{\text{total\_fare}}{\text{route\_median}}, \text{total\_fare} \right]\).

\[s(\mathbf{x}, n) = 2^{-\frac{E(h(\mathbf{x}))}{c(n)}} \quad \text{where} \quad c(n) = 2\left( \ln(n - 1) + 0.5772156649 \right) - \frac{2(n - 1)}{n}\]

Adaptive IQR Contamination: Contamination is bounded dynamically between \(0.005\) and \(0.035\) using \(\text{IQR} = Q_{75} - Q_{25}\) to avoid falsely purging festive holiday surges while reliably isolating ₹0 glitches, ₹99,999 bugs, and misclassified business-class seats.

3.8 Closed-Form Linear SHAP Explainability

MoSPI economists require transparent Rupee explainability without opaque neural network black boxes. Linear SHAP decomposes price movements into 4 causal factors:

\[\phi_j = \beta_j \left( x_j - E[X_j] \right) \quad \implies \quad \Delta \text{INR}_j = \left( \frac{\phi_j}{100} \right) \times P_{\text{base}}\]
Economic Driver Coefficient (\(\beta_j\)) Typical Impact Driver Description
ATF Jet Fuel Shock \(+0.32\) \(+₹300 \text{ to } +₹800\) Aviation Turbine Fuel price pass-through from crude indices
Festival Surge \(+15.50\) \(+₹600 \text{ to } +₹1,800\) High holiday and long-weekend consumer travel demand
Carrier Competition \(-3.80\) \(-₹250 \text{ to } -₹750\) Discount pressure per additional active carrier on the route
Booking Window Lead \(-1.15\) \(-₹400 \text{ to } -₹1,200\) Advance purchase discount per week of booking lead time

3.9 Route Monopoly Watchdog (Herfindahl-Hirschman Index)

\[\text{HHI}_r = \sum_{i=1}^{K} \left( s_{r,i} \times 100 \right)^2 \quad \text{where} \quad s_{r,i} = \frac{\text{Flights}_{r,i}}{\sum_j \text{Flights}_{r,j}}\]

Regulatory Thresholding: A route is flagged for automated DGCA & CCI intervention if:

\[\text{HHI}_r \ge 2500 \quad \mathbf{AND} \quad \frac{\text{Fare/km}_r}{\text{National Benchmark Fare/km}} \ge 1.25\]

Live Alert Example: BOM-IXU (Mumbai–Aurangabad) has \(\text{HHI} = 10,000\) (100% IndiGo monopoly) charging ₹16.7/km (+72% above regional benchmark), automatically generating a legal briefing dossier under Section 3(4) of the Competition Act 2002.
